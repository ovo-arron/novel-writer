## 1. Chinese webnovel and fiction practice

| Source | Transferable craft observation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 血红《网络小说人物的设定》 — <https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2021/0716/c404024-32159694.html> | A long plot accumulates as a character's life; behavior and choices establish a person more reliably than adjective lists | One author's long-form webnovel practice, not a rule for every light novel |
| 任怨《伴国漫之光，谈网文创作》 — <https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2021/0910/c441011-32223168.html> | Dialogue should fit identity, education, environment, and relationship; character change needs process | Natural speech is not automatically contemporary slang |
| 姚璎《文火慢功熬香粥》 — <https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2022/0506/c441011-32415170.html> | Observed labor, shared space, local speech, and material routine can develop plot and character together | Verify professional and dialect detail; do not pile facts for generic “smoke and fire” |
| 知白《万千错处，终有真我》 — <https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2022/0211/c441011-32349952.html> | Surface imitation cannot replace the writer's problem-solving; chapters may deliver emotional or character value without a plot climax | Local completeness does not require closing every question |
| 愤怒的香蕉《赘婿是一次实验》 — <https://www.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2022/0311/c441011-32372465.html> | Practice can use separate short-, medium-, and long-horizon goals; analyze results and control late-stage line expansion | Do not turn technical goals into a checklist displayed in every chapter |
| 天蚕土豆访谈 — <https://image.chinawriter.com.cn/n1/2023/0823/c404024-40062009.html> | Broad reading builds genre instinct; fantasy atmosphere, shared emotion, and satisfying reversals must be felt in events | Do not reduce emotion and payoff to slogans or a fixed humiliation ladder |

## 3. Humanization research

| Source | Use |
|---|---|
| Humanizer-zh — <https://github.com/op7418/Humanizer-zh> | General symptoms such as inflated significance, promotional evaluation, false ranges, formulaic triads, negative antithesis, vague authority, and generic conclusions |
| Wikipedia, “Signs of AI writing” — <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing> | A changing catalog of observable text tendencies; use as hypothesis generation, not authorship proof |
| WikiProject AI Cleanup — <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_AI_Cleanup> | Editorial examples of repetitive explanatory and formatting residue in non-fiction; adapt cautiously to fiction |

Fiction requires additional evidence: viewpoint ownership, character knowledge, relationship permission, material resistance, causal aftermath, and variation of precision. Never promise detector evasion or infer authorship from a phrase.

## 4. Safe transfer method

For any research source:

1. record the exact source and scope;
2. extract one observable craft claim;
3. state where it may not apply;
4. test it on an original scene with different people, setting, objects, conflict, and outcome;
5. retain the claim only when the revision improves specific evidence;
6. treat later author feedback as stronger evidence for that project.

Do not convert an interview, tutorial, or famous work into a surface-style prompt.

## 5. Skill-source roles

Use the three software-skill sources for different problems:

| Source | Useful contribution to NW | Do not inherit blindly |
|---|---|---|
| Webnovel Writer — <https://github.com/lingfengQAQ/webnovel-writer> | long-form planning, project continuity, genre routing, chapter NG, dialogue ownership, author feedback, and reference boundaries | runtime, database, platform workflow, every detailed inventory in one prompt |
| Humanizer-zh — <https://github.com/op7418/Humanizer-zh> | observable general AI-text symptoms and the need for voice rather than merely “clean” prose | authorship detection claims, universal word bans, non-fiction formatting rules applied mechanically to fiction |
| InkOS — <https://github.com/Narcooo/inkos> | intent/canon/focus/plan separation, task-scoped context, branch comparison, draft–audit–revise–settle phases | command surface, provider/runtime setup, analytics, detector scores, or automatic canon promotion |

NW's Core I consumes intent, causal reasoning, story dimensions, and references. Core II consumes a minimal context packet, prose craft, dialogue, and evidence-based repair. Core III retrieves and settles per-novel truth, working state, accepted craft evidence, and author-confirmed project methods.
