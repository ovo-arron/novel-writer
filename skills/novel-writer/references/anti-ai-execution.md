# Anti-AI Execution Router for Fiction

Use this reference when the author calls prose “AI,” “mechanical,” “too logical,” “engineered,” or “not something a person would say,” or when a large anti-mechanical pass is requested. This is a routing layer, not another symptom catalog and not an authorship detector.

## 1. Operating boundary

“AI-like” is a reader diagnosis that needs textual evidence. It may point to a word, but it often points deeper: a person speaks for the author, an action exists to move exposition, a body stops without cause, or a scene is assembled from functions rather than lived continuity.

Use this order:

```text
retrieve accepted project evidence → mark the exact reading failure
→ locate the deepest failed layer → run one matching repair
→ reread the affected span → stop when the evidence is gone
```

During first draft, choose at most one likely risk. During revision, do not load every NG table. A full workshop may report at most three high-impact craft categories and one dialogue category unless truth or continuity requires more.

## 2. Locate the deepest failed layer

Repair the highest row that genuinely fails. A deeper repair may make lower notes disappear.

| Priority | Failed layer | Observable evidence | First move |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Truth or continuity | time, knowledge, motive, weapon, wound, custody, route, or canon contradicts the accepted state | reopen authority and predecessor passage |
| 2 | Scene activity | people do a task mainly to enter a place, expose lore, overhear a clue, or display competence | rebuild what they would be doing in their own day |
| 3 | Action causality | attention, body, object, force, or position changes without a sufficient trigger | restore stimulus, contact, motion, and consequence |
| 4 | Spoken turn | the line answers the author's need rather than the listener's pressure, relationship, or knowledge | rebuild the whole turn from speaker ownership |
| 5 | Wording and rhythm | collocation, verb, object, clause order, paragraphing, or repetition is locally unnatural | make the smallest local rewrite |

Do not solve rows 1–3 with synonym replacement. Do not redesign a sound scene because one verb is awkward.

## 3. Six fast tests

Use only the tests needed to locate the layer.

1. **Rename test** — replace the prop, facility, lesson, or location with another plausible noun. If the same people perform the same convenient function, the scene activity is still wrong.
2. **Reader-known test** — if the reader already knew the delivered information, would the character still attend, inspect, buy, ask, or overhear? If not, rebuild the activity before the wording.
3. **Stimulus test** — is a name, glance, sound, pain, threat, or discovery strong enough for this person to stop, turn, lower a weapon, or change route now? If not, let motion continue or provide a concrete cause.
4. **Speaker-swap test** — can another age, job, status, or relationship say the line unchanged? If so, restore current aim, permission, knowledge, and lived vocabulary.
5. **Mouth-and-object test** — can this person say the words in this order, and can both listener and reader recover one clear omitted object? Semantic guessability alone does not pass.
6. **Deletion test** — remove the explanatory tail, action tag, final maxim, or catalog question. If action and meaning remain clear, leave it deleted instead of replacing it with a subtler flourish.

## 4. Route one repair

| Evidence points to | Read next | Repair target |
|---|---|---|
| canon, knowledge, body, weapon, time, spatial handoff | `continuity.md` | accepted truth and exact state |
| convenient activity, function-grid scene, tutorial, detached atmosphere, thesis ending | `natural-prose.md` and the relevant part of `drafting-engine.md` | lived activity, resistance, consequence |
| unclear force, movement, interruption, combat position | `drafting-engine.md`; use `continuity.md` for cross-chapter handoff | physical chain and tactical choice |
| stiff, over-logical, interchangeable, expositional speech | `dialogue.md` | speaker-owned turn and listener pressure |
| local collocation, connector chain, paragraph button, rhythm, formatting | `natural-prose.md` | smallest sentence or paragraph repair |
| author correction or repeated project failure | `revision.md` and `project-memory.md` | scoped learning with a next-use test |

Do not decorate a failed activity with weather, gestures, and ordinary objects. Do not preserve an entire rejected exchange by making each line more colloquial. Rebuild the failed layer first, then keep only local fixes still needed.

## 5. Chinese dialogue fast gate

For one suspicious turn, answer in this order:

1. What does the speaker want the listener to do, answer, stop, notice, or allow now?
2. What can this speaker say to this listener without losing face, rank, safety, or intimacy?
3. Which noun and verb would this household, job, age, and moment normally put together?
4. What reaches the mouth first: answer, request, refusal, complaint, warning, joke, or silence?
5. If something is omitted, is there exactly one nearby referent for both character and reader?

Then read the whole exchange aloud. Do not force every line short. A caregiver, teacher, official, trader, or frightened teenager may need a complete sentence for different reasons. Do not add particles, stutters, slang, ellipses, or action tags by quota.

For dialogue that keeps turning private understanding into polished explanation, use one positive conversion:

```text
private state, including confusion or self-deception → live target
→ listener model → permission and face/status risk
→ spoken remainder → what the listener actually takes up
```

The private state is evidence for selection, not a transcript to place in quotation marks. Preserve the noun, reason, or admission the speaker needs this listener to receive; leave the rest in action, timing, interiority, later contradiction, or genuine silence. The spoken remainder may be a complete argument when persuasion, duty, testimony, or record control requires one. Read `dialogue.md` for the full method and control example.

For group reactions, assign **response jurisdiction** before lines: who can take the body, wound, meal, payment, door, tool, child, record, explanation, or nothing. Equal concern does not require equal questions or page space.

For paragraphing, keep one paragraph while the same attention, action, speaker turn, or negotiation continues. Usually change paragraph when the speaker changes, but do not isolate every gesture or sentence into storyboard beats.

## 6. Humanizer boundary for fiction

Humanizer-style observations are useful for generating hypotheses: inflated significance, promotional evaluation, vague authority, forced triads, negative antithesis, synonym cycling, false ranges, excessive qualification, formatting residue, and generic positive conclusions.

In fiction, every proposed repair must still pass four gates:

- **viewpoint** — who owns this notice or judgment;
- **canon** — whether the specificity is established rather than invented;
- **relationship** — whether speech and response are socially available;
- **causality** — whether the change affects present action or aftermath.

Do not import general “humanization” recipes mechanically. Random first-person opinion, digression, error, slang, fragment, rough punctuation, mixed sentence length, or replacing every three-item list with two items can become a new formula. Preserve valid formality, direct emergency speech, deliberate repetition, quiet transitions, and ordinary complete sentences. Never promise detector evasion or infer authorship from a phrase.

## 7. Compile feedback without bloating NG

Store project feedback in Core III under one of three forms:

| Form | Evidence required | Store |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard ban** | the author explicitly bans an exact word, phrase, format, or implication within a stated project scope | exact form, scope, source, and any stated exception |
| **Conditional symptom** | a rejected example reveals a repeatable cause but the word can be valid elsewhere | surface locator, failed layer, observable reason, next-use test, and counterexample |
| **Liked evidence** | the author explicitly likes or accepts a line or mechanism | protected wording if requested, plus the transferable relationship or action mechanism |

Merge feedback that fails for the same cause. Do not store every changed word. A hard ban remains project-specific unless the author deliberately promotes it. A conditional symptom remains a test, not a blacklist. Generated alternatives do not become positive evidence until the author accepts them.

Use this compact record:

```text
source and scope → surface locator → failed/liked layer
→ observable cause → next-use test → counterexample → status
```

## 8. Regression and stop rule

After the repair, reread the whole exchange or the surrounding three paragraphs and check:

- accepted facts, positions, body, items, and knowledge still agree;
- the character remains distinct with this listener;
- necessary information is still recoverable;
- paragraph movement has not become uniformly short or uniformly polished;
- no new lore, motive, memory, danger, or clever ending was invented;
- the next action or aftermath still follows.

When the targeted evidence disappears and these checks pass, stop. Do not continue searching for more “human” irregularity. A clean ordinary sentence is allowed.
