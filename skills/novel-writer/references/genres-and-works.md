## 1. Use boundary

- Treat every entry as a comparative craft hypothesis, not an author statement.
- Named works may guide analysis; drafting receives anonymous targets such as “rules alter action immediately” or “dialogue combines bargaining with intimacy.”
- Do not reproduce plots, characters, powers, signature relationships, famous lines, paragraph shapes, catchphrases, or the distinctive style of a living author.
- Do not infer sentence-level style from summaries, adaptations, unofficial quotations, or memory.
- Separate Japanese originals, licensed Chinese translations, web versions, print revisions, animation scripts, and promotional copy.
- Use lawful author publications, publisher pages, licensed previews, or short samples supplied by the user. Do not seek pirated mirrors or store continuous source text.

Title aliases retained from the WW reference catalog: `《俺ガイル》` refers to `《我的青春恋爱物语果然有问题》`; `《实教》` refers to `《欢迎来到实力至上主义的教室》`; `《转生成为史莱姆》` refers to `《关于我转生变成史莱姆这档事》`; `《这个美好世界》` refers to `《为美好的世界献上祝福！》`. Treat aliases as the same work, not extra evidence.

## 1A. Universal genre and form map

The named catalog below reflects earlier fantasy and light-novel research; it is not NW's genre boundary. For any fiction project, begin with the transferable problem rather than searching for a superficially similar title.

| Form or genre | Central craft questions | Useful evidence in scene | Frequent false default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short story | What single pressure, omission, image, or choice can carry the whole work? | compressed state changes, echo, decisive selection | miniature novel outline |
| Novella | Which central arc deserves room, and which secondary material must remain implied? | sequences with cumulative consequence | padded short story or rushed novel |
| Contemporary/realist | How do work, money, care, family, status, body, and place constrain choice? | routine, labor, bills, travel, rooms, reputation | treating ordinary life as lack of plot |
| Literary/character-driven | How do attention, contradiction, memory, and form create consequence? | viewpoint selection, recurring material detail, moral cost | obscurity, plotlessness, uniformly polished prose |
| Romance | What do two people want from each other, fear granting, and risk changing? | invitations, touch, timing, refusal, care, public/private choices | compulsory beat sheet or miscommunication without motive |
| Mystery/crime | Who can know, access, alter, conceal, or misread each piece of evidence? | objects, records, testimony, opportunity, investigative cost | clue omniscience and solution monologue |
| Thriller | Which options and time are narrowing, and what competence still has a cost? | pursuit, preparation, failed contingencies, compromised choices | constant shouting or invulnerability |
| Horror | What boundary is violated, what remains uncertain, and what residue survives contact? | anticipation, bodily response, unreliable safety, changed routine | explanation quota or gore quota |
| Historical | What could these people plausibly know, do, say, own, and risk here? | material life, institutions, status, period constraints, source uncertainty | contemporary character in costume |
| Science fiction | How does one altered premise propagate through bodies, labor, institutions, and belief? | operational use, limit, maintenance, unintended adaptation | encyclopedia disguised as dialogue |
| Fantasy/speculative | Which invented difference changes ordinary action and distributes power? | use, cost, access, culture, consequence | mandatory taxonomy, map, quest, or chosen hero |
| War/political | How do command, legitimacy, logistics, propaganda, and civilian survival collide? | routes, supplies, delay, burial, bargaining, aftermath | maps or speeches standing in for lived war |
| Comedy/satire | Which stable desire, flaw, role, or institution repeatedly produces costly mismatch? | timing, status reversal, failed self-presentation, aftermath | interchangeable quips and consequence-free stupidity |
| Family/workplace/social | Which obligations and dependencies cannot be solved by simply leaving? | chores, schedules, authority, shared space, remembered favors | thematic speeches instead of role conflict |
| Series/serial | What pressure renews after each local settlement, and what reader memory needs support? | changed inheritance, obligations, evolving opposition | cliffhanger at every boundary |
| Nonlinear/documentary | Why is this information arriving in this order and through this artifact? | source, audience, access, alteration, retrospective change | chronology puzzle without emotional or causal gain |

Use `adaptation-router.md` to rank a primary promise, secondary promises, length/release mode, narrative form, language/register, and active domains. Then use named works only if comparative mechanisms would answer a remaining craft question.

## 2. Chinese progression, cultivation, and modern fantasy

| Work | Transferable mechanism | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|
| 《斗破苍穹》 | Make lack, humiliation, resource, rank, and near-term goal visible; turn progress into a concrete reversal of available action | signature powers, mentor structure, fixed ridicule–reveal–shock ladder, slogans |
| 《武动乾坤》 | Ground cultivation pressure in family livelihood and bodily effort; verify growth through tools, terrain, and opponents | crowd choruses, interchangeable tournament escalation, named-move spectacle without position |
| 《大主宰》 | Combine youth ensemble, academy or regional stages, readable factions, and forward-looking ambition | faction reskins, ornate move names replacing cost and space |
| 《凡人修仙传》 | Let resources, caution, travel, apprenticeship, and asymmetric danger shape choices; survival knowledge accumulates slowly | universal suspicion, endless inventory bookkeeping, exact advancement structures |
| 《仙逆》 | Allow choices and losses to harden worldview over long time; make power change moral and relational distance | signature tragic incidents, cultivation cosmology, aphoristic bleakness as a surface voice |
| 《牧神记》 | Reveal a large world through local myths, institutions, travel, and competing histories; keep curiosity active | its cosmology, village configuration, escalating revelation sequence |
| 《诡秘之主》 | Use profession, ritual, organization, evidence, and information cost to layer mysteries; clues alter risk before full explanation | pathways, rituals, organizations, Victorian surface package, exact mystery architecture |
| 《龙族》 | Place ordinary inadequacy beside an expensive, dangerous hidden world; route grandeur through private longing to be chosen | academy, dragon system, brand display, relationship map, signature lyrical declarations |

### Cultivation / progression target

Use clear desire, visible limits, resource consequence, and repeated proof of growth. The protagonist's competence should create new obligations and stronger opposition, not merely larger numbers. Let everyday costs and relationship changes interrupt the clean advancement ladder.

## 3. Japanese light-novel fantasy and isekai

| Work | Transferable mechanism | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|
| 《刀剑神域》 | Make rules immediately change survival action and relationships; convert a high concept into a personal choice | game UI dumps, signature skills, perfect-protagonist surface traits |
| 《Re:从零开始的异世界生活》 | Let failure accumulate as fear, shame, wrong coping, and changed understanding; an ability does not equal mastery | return-by-death mechanism, repeated torture as emotional shorthand, translated catchphrases |
| 《关于我转生变成史莱姆这档事》 | Turn protection, naming, building, and administration into visible social growth; keep large change readable through practical decisions | species, skills, city, naming system, universal admiration, meeting-minutes exposition |
| 《无职转生》 | Use bodily limits, language learning, household life, old flaws, and long consequences to make reincarnation developmental | family structure, eroticized age contrast, biography, world system, first-person surface rhythm |
| 《记录的地平线》 | Contrast remembered rules with lived reality; let group coordination become institutional design | MMO classes, city and guild structure, strategist archetype |
| 《本好きの下剋上》 | Let body, class, family permission, labor, materials, and literacy make knowledge costly to apply | bookmaking sequence, family arrangement, inventions, modern-superiority framing |
| 《蜘蛛ですが、なにか？》 | Let self-talk manage fear while material conditions repeatedly correct the character's model | monster species, skill menu, comedic monologue cadence |
| 《OVERLORD》 | Use asymmetric power to explore organization, misinterpretation, and the distance between inner uncertainty and public authority | characters, guild origin, servants, conquest sequence, intimidating speech patterns |
| 《幼女战记》 | Create irony between rational self-interest, bureaucracy, doctrine, and how institutions interpret success | reincarnation premise, military campaigns, ideological duel, formal monologue voice |
| 《狼与香辛料》 | Make dialogue simultaneously trade, test, flirtation, and risk allocation; professional knowledge enters through winning and losing | archaic verbal tics, pair dynamics, economics lectures, constant cleverness |
| 《为美好的世界献上祝福！》 | Build comedy from stable flaws colliding; let undignified solutions work and create aftermath | party composition, character labels, recurring gags, indiscriminate incompetence |
| 《在地下城寻求邂逅是否搞错了什么》 | Convert admiration into training and choices; track dungeon position, resources, retreat, and rescue | level system, harem reward structure, signature skills |
| 《哥布林杀手》 | Emphasize preparation, professional coordination, limited knowledge, and the local cost of violence | monster premise, party composition, ritualized terse voice, graphic repetition |
| 《十二国记》 | Let political legitimacy, social responsibility, displacement, and self-knowledge develop through institutions and travel | world structure, rulers, creatures, specific exile-to-rule arc |
| 《奇诺之旅》 | Use compact journeys to test values without forcing one moral answer; let local rules expose their own cost | traveler setup, vehicle companion, country-of-the-week formulas |
| 《来自新世界》 | Let childhood normality gradually expose institutional control and morally compromised survival | social system, powers, revelation sequence, final twist structure |
| 《银河英雄传说》 | Give strategy political consequence and opposing sides intelligent futures; separate command perception from civilian cost | historical scale, factions, commanders, campaigns, documentary voice |
| 《86—不存在的战区—》 | Use spatial separation, communication delay, equipment, names, and routine to reveal institutional violence | unit configuration, weapons, ethnic system, declarations, tragedy styling |
| 《灰与幻想的格林姆迦尔》 | Let novice weakness, equipment cost, group labor, hesitation, and grief alter later coordination instead of disappearing after a victory | party composition, world premise, signature deaths, melancholy surface cadence |
| 《无头骑士异闻录 DuRaRaRa!!》 | Build an ensemble network in which rumors, aliases, private aims, and partial viewpoints make one city event mean different things | cast, gangs, urban legends, exact reveal order, hyperactive narrative surface |
| 《不吉波普不笑》 | Revisit one pressure through separated viewpoints and delayed recontextualization; let a later account change earlier meaning | title persona, school incident, chronology, reveal device, recognizable voice |
| 《药屋少女的呢喃》 | Let professional knowledge enter through assigned work, access limits, evidence, hierarchy, and the risk of being noticed | palace arrangement, cases, character pairing, poisons, deduction sequence |

### Light-novel fantasy target

Keep viewpoint close, rules actionable, interaction lively, and exposition attached to failure, work, bargaining, travel, or risk. Write idiomatic Chinese rather than simulated translation. Humor may sit beside danger, but cannot erase cost.

## 3A. Isekai mechanism collision map

The following works are often requested together, but their useful mechanisms should not be averaged into one surface voice.

| Craft problem | Comparative mechanism | Incompatibility to decide before drafting |
|---|---|---|
| Failure changes the person | 《Re:从零开始的异世界生活》: failure memory accumulates fear, shame, damaged trust, and altered coping | Do not pair severe residue with effortless competence recovery or universal reassurance in the same sequence |
| Growth becomes social order | 《关于我转生变成史莱姆这档事》: protection and competence become allocation, work, administration, and obligation | Fast readable building can conflict with prolonged bodily helplessness; choose which owns the current arc |
| Danger remains spatial | 《刀剑神域》: rules, distance, tools, experience gaps, and body feedback change immediate choices | Clarity must not become UI narration, skill rotation, or a flawless-protagonist surface |
| Reincarnation remains development | 《无职转生》: body, language, household relations, old flaws, and time limit imported knowledge | Developmental slowness conflicts with instant modern superiority and frictionless mastery |
| Knowledge meets material life | 《本好きの下剋上》: class, labor, materials, illness, money, and family permission make ideas costly | Material friction must alter plans; it cannot be a decorative poverty montage before easy invention |
| Self-talk meets survival | 《蜘蛛ですが、なにか？》: self-explanation manages fear until hunger, route, body, and danger correct it | High-density comic thought can weaken external pressure if it never returns to action |
| Remembered rules meet institutions | 《记录的地平线》: old knowledge is revised by bodies, groups, status, procedure, and political consequence | Institutional reasoning can become meeting-minutes exposition unless competing interests act independently |

Choose two to four works that answer different questions. State which mechanism leads, which one only supports, and what cannot coexist in the same sequence. Then remove all work names before drafting. A **reference salad** is present when titles can be recognized from copied surface features but the project's own human pressure remains unchanged.

## 4. Youth, school, and relationship-centered references

| Work | Transferable mechanism | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|
| 《我的青春恋爱物语果然有问题》 | A first-person observer can be sharp and still defend their own wound; kindness may use unpleasant wording | toxic-wit syntax, club setup, relationship triangle, omniscient cynicism |
| 《欢迎来到实力至上主义的教室》 | Make rules, reputation, alliance, and information operational; let institutions create strategic pressure | school system, hidden-mastermind reveal pattern, retrospective omniscience |
| 《冰菓》 | Let small objects, errors, and ordinary talk invite inference; observation precedes conclusion | energy-saving persona, club configuration, turning every day into a grand mystery |
| 《青春猪头少年》系列 | Use restrained joking to shelter real concern; let a fantasy disturbance refract a social or emotional problem | couple banter rhythm, supernatural cases, diagnostic dialogue |
| 《龙与虎》 | Ground relationships in food, cleaning, family space, class, and embarrassing physical aftermath | violent-tsundere routine, character arrangement, loudness without domestic life |
| 《凉宫春日》系列 | Let a narrator's resistance set the scale of abnormal events; ordinary group routine can contain extreme stakes | club setup, character attributes, narrator voice, episode structures |
| 《文学少女》系列 | Let reading, interpretation, appetite, and confession connect texts to private harm without making literature a solution key | character setup, book-by-book mystery formula, quoted-text dependency |

### Youth target

Make social rules and ordinary places matter. Smart observation needs a blind spot. Attraction, resentment, and loyalty should alter invitations, timing, seating, messages, chores, and public choices rather than appearing only as inner analysis.

## 5. Serious fantasy and war

Combine references rather than copying one surface:

- Use 《86》 for distance between protected observers and expendable bodies.
- Use 《银河英雄传说》 for strategic alternatives and political consequence.
- Use 《幼女战记》 for bureaucracy turning private logic into institutional momentum.
- Use 《哥布林杀手》 for preparation, local expertise, and the cost of underestimating repeated violence.
- Use 《十二国记》 for legitimacy, administration, and responsibility after victory.
- Use 《来自新世界》 for institutions that preserve life through morally compromised rules.

War becomes serious through work and interruption: transport, burial, rationing, medicine, missing labor, delayed orders, equipment reuse, desertion, refugees, civilian bargains, and veterans who remain necessary after their heroism has become inconvenient.

## 6. Urban occult and hidden-world fantasy

Combine:

- 《龙族》 — private longing beneath a glamorous dangerous world;
- 《诡秘之主》 — organizations, professions, rituals, evidence, and information cost;
- 《冰菓》 — observation before conclusion at the scale of ordinary objects;
- 《青春猪头少年》 — fantasy pressure refracting social and emotional conflict.

Keep the mundane world capable of hurting the protagonist. Secret power should not erase rent, family, shame, transport, records, school, work, or the need to be believed.

## 8. Fingerprint method

When analyzing a lawful sample, record:

- exact version, language, URL, and sample scope;
- viewpoint distance;
- sentence-group movement;
- exposition carrier;
- dialogue dynamics;
- how emotion changes action;
- scene exit;
- one anonymous transferable target;
- one anti-copy boundary;
- confidence and blind spots.

Write an original control sample with different characters, objects, conflict, setting, and outcome. If readers can immediately reconstruct the source scene, abstract further.

## 9. Passing a reference into drafting

Use this shape:

```text
Viewpoint target: close third person with fallible self-explanation.
Scene movement: judgment → material evidence → correction → pressured choice.
Dialogue target: professional knowledge enters through bargaining, not lecture.
Exit: a practical consequence interrupts the character's preferred interpretation.
Avoid: catchphrases, source-specific props, plot structures, and sentence-shape imitation.
```

Remove work and author names before drafting.

## 10. Reference routing by novel dimension

Do not load all works because a project is “fantasy.” Select two to four references that illuminate different dimensions, convert them into anonymous targets, then draft from the project's own canon.

| Dimension | Useful comparative cluster | Question to extract |
|---|---|---|
| Countries, legitimacy, administration | 《十二国记》《银河英雄传说》《幼女战记》《86—不存在的战区—》 | How do institutions turn belief, command, law, distance, and unequal protection into daily consequences? |
| Economy, labor, class, settlement | 《狼与香辛料》《本好きの下剋上》《关于我转生变成史莱姆这档事》《凡人修仙传》 | What material chain makes knowledge, growth, protection, and mobility costly? |
| Long causal plot and mystery | 《诡秘之主》《Re:从零开始的异世界生活》《来自新世界》《欢迎来到实力至上主义的教室》 | How do evidence, failure, information access, and earlier choices alter later options? |
| Growth without instant mastery | 《无职转生》《Re:从零开始的异世界生活》《十二国记》《仙逆》 | Which old strategy fails, what change costs, and how does later behavior prove memory? |
| Items, tools, preparation, and resources | 《凡人修仙传》《哥布林杀手》《本好きの下剋上》《刀剑神域》 | How do custody, preparation, material limits, maintenance, and use change outcomes? |
| Creatures, species, and nonhuman identity | 《蜘蛛ですが、なにか？》《关于我转生变成史莱姆这档事》《OVERLORD》《来自新世界》 | How do body, classification, society, ecology, and political interpretation constrain nonhuman life? |
| Distinct character voice and relationships | 《我的青春恋爱物语果然有问题》《冰菓》《龙与虎》《青春猪头少年》《狼与香辛料》 | What does each person notice, risk, refuse, and ask from this listener? |
| Ensemble and faction dynamics | 《记录的地平线》《银河英雄传说》《大主宰》《凉宫春日》系列 | How do groups retain separate interests instead of orbiting one protagonist? |
| Multi-view network and recontextualization | 《无头骑士异闻录 DuRaRaRa!!》《不吉波普不笑》《银河英雄传说》 | Which viewpoint owns each fact, and what later account changes an earlier meaning or available action? |
| Profession, evidence, and access | 《药屋少女的呢喃》《狼与香辛料》《本好きの下剋上》《诡秘之主》 | How does skilled work create access, risk, evidence, obligation, and unwanted visibility? |
| Fragile group growth and aftermath | 《灰与幻想的格林姆迦尔》《86—不存在的战区—》《哥布林杀手》 | Which bodily, material, and relational costs change how the group prepares and trusts next time? |
| Progression and serial momentum | 《斗破苍穹》《武动乾坤》《大主宰》《在地下城寻求邂逅是否搞错了什么》 | What near-term lack is visible, how is progress verified, and what larger obligation follows success? |
| Humor beside danger | 《为美好的世界献上祝福！》《蜘蛛ですが、なにか？》《青春猪头少年》《凉宫春日》系列 | Which stable need or relationship mismatch creates comedy without erasing consequence? |
| Urban hidden world | 《龙族》《诡秘之主》《冰菓》《青春猪头少年》系列 | How does the extraordinary remain entangled with rent, records, family, school, shame, and belief? |
| War, logistics, and civilian cost | 《86—不存在的战区—》《银河英雄传说》《幼女战记》《哥布林杀手》 | What do maps omit, and which bodies, routes, supplies, duties, and political interests carry the war? |

Reference breadth is not quality by itself. If two sources suggest incompatible mechanisms, state the tradeoff instead of blending their surfaces. The project decides which target matters.

## 11. Use works differently for conception and prose

| Stage | Lawful evidence | Safe output |
|---|---|---|
| Conceive premise, setting, system, faction, or plot | broad structure, author interviews, official synopsis, reliable edition identity, lawful primary text | anonymous mechanism, tradeoff, causal hypothesis, anti-copy boundary |
| Calibrate scene movement, narration, dialogue, rhythm, or wording | author-published text, licensed preview, official excerpt, or user-provided lawful sample in the relevant language/version | viewpoint distance, sentence-group movement, information carrier, dialogue dynamics, original control sample |
| Draft manuscript | project canon plus anonymous targets | original prose with no source names or surface imitation |

Do not infer sentence-level style from adaptations, quote compilations, short-video edits, fan summaries, translations of unknown provenance, or remembered famous lines. A licensed translation can inform that translation's Chinese effect; it does not prove the Japanese original uses the same syntax.

During conception, triangulate works that solve different problems. During drafting, use at most one or two prose targets that the current scene can actually support. Loading many titles at once tends to produce recognizable trope collage or uniform “light-novel flavor” rather than a coherent original voice.
